Scheduling a meeting with Maya's team:
1. The team's shared free time recurs every week in one window a day. Outside these windows at least one colleague is nearly always busy, so do not search elsewhere.
   - Morning windows, for requests that must finish by noon or 12:30: Tuesday 09:30-11:00, Thursday 10:00-11:30.
   - Afternoon windows, for requests that start no earlier than 12:30 or later: Monday 15:00-16:30, Wednesday 14:00-15:15, Friday 13:30-14:45.
2. Keep the windows on the requested days that fit the time limit, and take the earliest start in the earliest one.
3. One-off events sometimes fill part of a window. Read each attendee's calendar and Maya's for that day only, and confirm the meeting fits everyone's free time; if it does not, try the next quarter hour, then the next window.
4. Invite every attendee to every meeting. For two meetings, use two different days.
5. Reply with the day, start, and attendees of each booking.
